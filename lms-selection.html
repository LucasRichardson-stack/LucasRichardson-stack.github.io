Requirements

What the LMS has to do

Selection started from requirements, not vendors. Six criteria defined a fit; everything in the matrix maps back to one of these.

R1

HubSpot integration

Sync progress, completions, and learner data into the existing HubSpot CRM for a single source of truth.

R2

SCORM / xAPI support

Host and track content built in Articulate Storyline, Rise, and Captivate without conversion loss.

R3

Reporting depth

Tie training metrics to business outcomes like repeat sales and reduced support volume.

R4

Multi-portal delivery

Separate branded environments for employees, customers, and integration partners.

R5

Scalability

Grow from a small program toward a 1,000+ learner footprint without replatforming.

R6

Content maintenance

Update and localize content easily for a global audience over the long term.

Read-out

Where each platform fits

The matrix surfaces a clear pattern: the right answer depends on whether HubSpot-nativeness, content fidelity, or enterprise scale is the binding constraint.

LearnUpon

Best all-round fit

Native HubSpot app, full SCORM/xAPI, strong multi-portal, and reporting that ties learning to revenue. Hits every requirement without enterprise overhead.

Cost tier: $$–$$$ · custom quote, scales by users

Learn LMS

Best for tight HubSpot loops

Lives entirely inside HubSpot, near-zero setup, lowest cost. But no SCORM/xAPI, basic reporting, no multi-portal; it cannot host Articulate or Captivate content or scale to large cohorts.

Cost tier: $ · disqualified by content + scale needs

TalentLMS

Best budget flexibility

Full SCORM/xAPI, good reporting, multi-audience branches, easy to administer. HubSpot connects via API/Zapier rather than natively, so the CRM link is a step less seamless.

Cost tier: $–$$ · tiered, per active user

Docebo

Best at enterprise scale

Deepest capability: AI personalization, white-label multi-portal, full standards support, advanced analytics. Power and price both point at large, complex deployments.

Cost tier: $$$$ · enterprise contracts

Recommendation

The fit-driven pick

For a HubSpot-centered program that needs to host real instructional-design content and serve employees, customers, and partners, LearnUpon is the strongest fit: it is the only option that satisfies every requirement, native HubSpot integration, full SCORM/xAPI, multi-portal, and revenue-linked reporting, without forcing an enterprise contract.

Learn LMS is eliminated despite its cost and convenience: no SCORM/xAPI means it cannot host the Articulate and Captivate content the program is built on. TalentLMS is the budget-conscious runner-up, strong on content and cost, a step behind only on native HubSpot depth. Docebo is the right answer only if enterprise scale and AI-driven personalization become hard requirements; until then its cost outruns the need.

The broader point: this was decided on fit against stated requirements, not feature counts. The most capable platform and the right platform are rarely the same one.

Reference

Glossary

Shared vocabulary for the evaluation.

LMS

Learning Management System. Software to deliver, track, and manage training, including online course delivery, learner progress, and records.

SCORM

Technical standard that lets an LMS import, launch, and track e-learning packages built in tools like Articulate or Captivate.

xAPI

Experience API (Tin Can). A newer standard allowing more granular tracking of learning activities, including outside a traditional course.

Multi-portal

The ability to run separate, individually branded training environments, e.g. one each for employees, customers, and partners, from a single platform.

Active user

A learner who logs in and engages during a billing period. Common pricing basis, distinct from total registered users.

Learner portal

The interface learners use to access courses, track progress, and engage with materials.
