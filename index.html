About

I'm a training and L&D leader based in Michigan. I design programs people actually use on the job: labs, certifications, LMS operations, and sales enablement for engineering and commercial teams in manufacturing and SaaS.

Recently at Apera AI, I was hired to build the training department and course catalog from scratch. Before that I was Regional Training Manager for North America at Universal Robots, leading a distributed team and partner network across the U.S. and Canada. At Kawasaki Robotics I built technical courses and moved the catalog online with digital twin simulation. At Liberty Robotics I created the company's training program from the ground up.

I started my career with the United States Peace Corps in Kyrgyzstan, where I learned adult education principals and specialized in train-the-trainer projects. I have a MS in Management from UIUC (Gies). Outside work I run, practice yoga, and volunteer with The Greening of Detroit and local animal rescues.

AI-assisted Instructional design
Training Planner

An L&D tool I built to help my team design new training courses. Storyboarding and planning in one place: workflow breakdowns, module design checklists, and module flow maps. The storyboard breaks each module into activities, content, quizzes, videos, and other screen types. I was challenged to use AI to help decrease training production time team workload. This was a simple solution and has helped drop development time by about 5%.

  • Module storyboards by screen type (activity, content, quiz, video, and more)
  • Planning views: design checklist, workflow breakdown, module flow
  • Course and module setup with optional frameworks (e.g. LIFT)
