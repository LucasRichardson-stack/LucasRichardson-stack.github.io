Apera AI Training Roadmap

A 12-month plan to build a training function from the ground up at an AI-driven robotic vision company, delivered as Training Lead within the first weeks in the role. The mandate: stand up a credible, scalable program where none existed.

The Mandate

The company had no training function. This roadmap, delivered in my first weeks, sequences the first year: earn credibility and standardize onboarding quickly, then scale curriculum and embed training cross-functionally, then institutionalize it as a global, certified, strategic differentiator.

The Plan

Three phases over twelve months

Each phase has a distinct objective and three workstreams. Front-load credibility and quick wins; scale in the middle; institutionalize at the end.

0–3 Months

Foundation & Quick Wins

Earn credibility; standardize onboarding

Assess & Audit

  • Map current materials. Inventory existing training, delivery methods, and gaps for the two flagship products.
  • Benchmark competitors. Compare training programs from leading machine-vision vendors.
  • Identify gaps. Pinpoint where current offerings fail customers, integrators, and partners.
  • Evaluate delivery. Analyze in-person, webinar, and online methods in use today.

Build Credibility

  • Deliver an executive session. Demonstrate the ROI link between training and business outcomes to secure leadership buy-in.
  • Shadow the work. Sit with integrators and operators on live projects to capture real needs and pain points.

Standardize Onboarding

  • Starter curriculum. Core safety, troubleshooting, and best practices for operators and integrators.
  • Train-the-trainer pilot. Establish a scalable model with the existing trainers, grounded in adult-learning principles.
3–6 Months

Scale & Integrate

Expand curriculum; embed cross-functionally

Curriculum Expansion

  • Tailored tracks. Distinct paths for operators, integrators, and sales/leadership, each addressing its own needs.
  • E-learning for scale. On-demand video, interactive simulations, and self-paced courses for 24/7 global access.

Cross-Functional Integration

  • Embed in the lifecycle. Work with Sales and CS on pre-sales demos and post-implementation coaching.
  • Partner with Engineering. Build training into product releases via documentation and release webinars.

Measurement & Feedback

  • Adopt an evaluation standard. Implement the Kirkpatrick four-level model (reaction, learning, behavior, results).
  • Build dashboards. Link training adoption to customer-success metrics: fewer troubleshooting calls, faster deployment.
6–12 Months

Global Impact & Innovation

Institutionalize; scale globally

Global Reach

  • Certification program. Validate integrator expertise to ensure consistent, quality deployments worldwide.
  • Regional expansion. Localized delivery across North America, Europe, and Asia-Pacific.

Innovation in Delivery

  • VR/AR & simulation. Immersive training for complex tasks like bin picking, sorting, and assembly.
  • Community knowledge hub. A central home for training videos, troubleshooting guides, and best practices.

Institutionalize & Scale

  • Formalize metrics. Adoption rates, customer ROI, reduced downtime as standing KPIs.
  • Training as a differentiator. Embed it in the sales cycle as a competitive advantage.
  • Career pathways. Build progression for the training team to retain top talent.

Enabling Infrastructure

What the function needs to run

A program is only as good as the infrastructure behind it. These are the standing investments the roadmap depends on.

LMS & website integration

A central platform for hosting, delivering, and tracking all training, integrated with the company website.

Instructional designer

Dedicated e-learning capability to build engaging video, simulations, and assessments for global, on-demand access.

Trainers

A team of SMEs and experienced trainers to deliver hands-on, in-person sessions on safety, troubleshooting, and best practices.

Travel training kits

Portable demo units, tools, and accessories for on-site training with customers and partners worldwide.

Third-party certification

Work with external bodies (e.g. SACA, NIMS) to validate the program for school and workforce-development use.
